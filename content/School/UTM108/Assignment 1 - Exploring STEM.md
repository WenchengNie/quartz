# Assignment 1: Independent - Exploring STEM (10%)

## Overview

Critical readings on sense of place (Gruenewald, 2003) and power in STEM learning (Weinstein et al., 2016) lead into this assignment. Submit **4 visuals** of STEM topics or socio-scientific issues (SSIs) in your local community, each with a caption. The critical visualizing activity connects learning to real-world applications.

## Requirements

- [ ] 4 images/photos, each worth 5%
- [ ] Use PowerPoint or other presentation software: one photo plus caption per slide
- [ ] Caption length: **20-60 words** each
- [ ] Include **location, date, and time** of image capture
- [ ] Individual submission
- [ ] No references required

## Rubric (total /20, then halved)

| Criteria | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Representation**: effectively meets the task by sharing 4 images; summarizes the STEM topic or SSI in your own words and shares your thinking; where applicable includes multiple perspectives or solutions, or questions for further inquiry/research (3 pts per image) | /12 |
| **Conceptual Understanding**: appreciates and understands the concept and shows evidence of STEM skills in real-world applications by connecting Science/STEM to a local object or experience (1 pt per image) | /4 |
| **Communication**: clarity, organization, and structure; appropriate use of ICT such as PPT; includes location, date, and time of capture (1 pt per image) | /4 |
| **Total** | /20 |

$$\text{Final grade} = \frac{\text{total}}{2} \quad (\text{out of } 10\%)$$
